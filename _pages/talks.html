My presentations and training contributions cover medical textiles, metamaterials, composite mechanics and multiscale modelling. Selected conference presentations are listed below, followed by technical training and earlier research seminars.

Selected conference presentations

Multiphysics Modelling of Embedded Fibre-Optic Strain Sensor in Knitted Textile Structure for Safety and Health Applications

E-Textiles 2026 — Accepted poster P40
Pilsen, Czech Republic · 24–26 November 2026

Co-authored contribution on the modelling of fibre-optic sensing within knitted textiles for protective and biomedical applications.

Multiscale Mechanical Modelling of Medical Textile for Female Pelvic Organ Repair

WCCM–ECCOMAS 2026 — Oral presentation
Munich, Germany · 19–24 July 2026
