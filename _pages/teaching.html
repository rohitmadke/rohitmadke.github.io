My teaching and supervision cover finite element methods, structural mechanics and computational modelling of textiles and protective systems. I have supported undergraduate and postgraduate teaching at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee and supervise research at Aix-Marseille University.

Research supervision

At the Laboratory of Applied Biomechanics (LBA), Aix-Marseille University, I supervise postgraduate research within the MICH Chair Innovative Materials for the Human Body, led by Prof. Frédéric Turquier.

Doctoral co-supervision

I co-supervise doctoral research on in-situ strain measurement in protective helmets with Prof. Frédéric Turquier.

Master's research supervision

Research topics include:

  • Yarn-level finite element modelling of knitted medical textiles.
  • Experimental textile characterisation and measurement of pore deformation.
  • Fibre-optic helmet modelling and strain sensing.

My supervisory work includes reviewing modelling assumptions, numerical methods and the interpretation of results with students.
