My research concerns the mechanics of textiles, composites and architected cellular materials, with applications in biomechanics and impact protection. I use nonlinear finite element analysis, multiscale modelling and experimental comparison, drawing on research appointments in France, the United States and India.

## Research and professional experience

### Postdoctoral Researcher

**Laboratory of Applied Biomechanics (LBA), Aix-Marseille University, France**  
September 2025–present

MICH Chair *Innovative Materials for the Human Body*, led by Prof. Frédéric Turquier.

- Develop multiscale models of knitted medical textiles, accounting for large deformation and frictional yarn contact; compare mechanical response and pore deformation with experimental measurements.
- Connect yarn-scale behaviour with homogenised fabric models for pelvic organ prolapse repair.
- Simulate textile-reinforced foam helmet liners in LS-DYNA under normal and oblique impacts, examining energy absorption and linear and angular head acceleration.
- Contribute to e-textile multiphysics studies involving mechanical deformation, fibre Bragg grating sensors and strain-transfer requirements for protective and medical applications.
- Supervise master's research and co-supervise doctoral work on in-situ helmet strain measurement.

### Research Engineer and Postdoctoral Fellow

**Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, India**  
October 2024–August 2025

- Investigated nonlinear deformation and energy absorption in chiral, achiral, auxetic and dual-negative metamaterials, including bistable and multistable designs.
- Performed topology optimisation using **LS-TaSC** and studied architected cellular structures for protective applications.
- Produced additively manufactured prototypes to investigate geometry-dependent mechanical response.

### Postdoctoral Research Scholar

**Department of Aerospace Engineering, San Diego State University, USA**  
April 2023–September 2024

U.S. Army Research Laboratory-funded research with Prof. Satchi Venkataraman.

- Developed finite element models of hybrid carbon-fibre-reinforced polymer (CFRP)/steel bolted joints, comparing model fidelity, predictive accuracy and computational cost.
- Investigated progressive damage, static and fatigue failure, and stacking-sequence effects; compared simulations with joint tests.
- Implemented constitutive and damage models in Abaqus through Fortran UMAT/VUMAT subroutines and developed Python simulation workflows.

### Senior Research Fellow

**Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, India**  
November 2020–March 2023

- Analysed heat-resistant sandwich structures with graphite auxetic cores and semi-auxetic carbon–carbon composites for an Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)-funded project.
- Investigated fracture using XFEM in Abaqus and phase-field methods in COMSOL Multiphysics and FEniCS.

### Research Associate

**Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, India**

### Project Engineer (Civil)

## Education

### PhD in Civil Engineering — Structural Engineering

**Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, India**  
2015–2022

*Multiscale Modelling and Failure Prediction of Braided Metacomposite*  
Supervisor: Prof. Rajib Chowdhury.

Doctoral work covered multiscale modelling of braided composites, auxetic and dual-negative metamaterials, metacomposite sandwich structures and ballistic-impact mitigation in multilayered body-armour concepts. The dual-negative architectures combined auxetic behaviour with negative stiffness. The work also included COMSOL studies of elastic-wave propagation and bandgaps in periodic metamaterials.

## Technical expertise

- **Mechanics and numerical methods:** nonlinear finite element analysis, explicit dynamics, contact, multiscale homogenisation, impact, progressive damage, fracture and fatigue, topology optimisation, elastic-wave propagation and bandgap analysis.
- **Simulation:** LS-DYNA, Abaqus, Ansys Mechanical/Workbench, ACP, Autodyn, COMSOL Multiphysics and LS-TaSC.
- **Programming:** Python, MATLAB and Fortran; UMAT/VUMAT implementation for constitutive and damage models.
- **Geometry, meshing and post-processing:** SpaceClaim, HyperMesh and LS-PrePost.
- **Textile and multiscale tools:** TexGen, TEXMIND and Digimat.
